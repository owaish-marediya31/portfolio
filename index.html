About

A quiet obsession
with getting it right

Good work speaks softly. It doesn’t need to shout — it simply holds up.

I’m Owaish Marediya, a web developer who enjoys building websites that feel clean, deliberate, and effortless to use. Front-end is where I’m most at home — shaping interfaces that work beautifully on every screen.

I’m currently deepening my back-end skills, having already shipped academic and client projects that span both sides of the stack. I believe the best way to learn is to build something real — so that’s exactly what I do.
